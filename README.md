## Repository

### Youtube Link

#### Description
This code turns 2D backgrounds into a 3D scrolling parallax background. This can be used in side scrolling games, platformer, or personal projects. By using the left and right arrow buttons, the background will scroll accordingly. The layers farther from the camera will scroll slower than the layers closer to the camera. 
Additionally, a pause menu has been added. By pressing the ESC button, the player can pause the game. By doing this, the ability to scroll left or right will be unavailable. Once paused, the player can either choose to continue or quit the game. 
Music has also been added to play in the background to enhance the player experience. 

##### Future Areas of Improvement 
- Having the music pause when the game is paused. Currently, the music plays through the pause menu. An added feature can either be pausing the music or have the music change when the pause menu is up. 
- Adding sound effects when the mouse hovers over the pause menu buttons. Currently, there is no sound when the options are hovered over or selected. Adding effects will enhance the experience for the player.
